What it is

Modern people are starved of love and no longer know what it is: they mistake sex for love, desire for love, companionship for love. OpenLove teaches what love actually is, in the ancient teaching way. Its definition instrument is the Fish Test: a man who says “I love fish” loves eating fish — from the fish's side, that “love” is death. Love is verified from the beloved's side: does the beloved, because of this love, live better, happier, freer? Its curriculum core: how to turn love from a feeling into a system — for family, partners, friends, strangers, enemies, oneself. OpenLove is the highest hall of OpenSchool, sibling of Dawn Buddha — the mission's positive curriculum.

END GOAL

Love turned into a runnable process no longer depends on mood and never goes out of supply — suffering is relieved BY love, operationalised. Love, run daily and automatically, for everyone the beloved's side can verify.
